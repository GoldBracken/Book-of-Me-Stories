What is Book of Me Stories?

Book of Me Stories is a website built to share your stories, your conversion, and your experiences with family. Now and for future generations.

Start Now!

How to get started

  1. Create your account to get started
  2. Write and share your personal stories
  3. View and manage your story collection
  4. Share memories with your family

Create your first story in 4 easy steps

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Click "Create a Story" from the menu.
  3. Fill in your story details and save.
  4. Share with family or keep it private.
